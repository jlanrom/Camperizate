2. What data we process and why

We process the data you give us when you contact us through the contact form, WhatsApp, phone or email (name, email, phone and the details of your enquiry) solely to reply to your request and, if you hire us, to manage the business relationship and invoicing.

3. Legal basis

The consent you give us when you send your enquiry (art. 6.1.a GDPR) and, where applicable, the performance of a contract or pre-contractual steps at your request (art. 6.1.b GDPR), as well as compliance with legal obligations such as tax obligations (art. 6.1.c GDPR).

4. Retention

We keep enquiry data for as long as needed to deal with it and, at most, one year if you do not become a customer. Customer data is kept for the duration of the business relationship and the legal periods that follow.

5. Recipients

We do not share your data with third parties unless required by law. To provide the service we use providers acting as data processors: Web3Forms, which receives the form messages and forwards them to us by email, and Cloudflare, which hosts this website. Some of these providers may process data outside the European Economic Area with the safeguards provided for in the GDPR (standard contractual clauses or adequacy frameworks).

6. Your rights

You can exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability by writing to info@lifescampers.com or by post to C/ Pascal, 14 · 29004 Málaga, Spain, stating which right you wish to exercise. If you believe we have not handled your request properly, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es).
