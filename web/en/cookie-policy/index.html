Cookie policy

This is an English translation for information purposes. In the event of any discrepancy, the Spanish version prevails.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small files that some websites store in your browser to remember information about your visit.

Cookies used on this website

This website does not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and does not include third-party videos, maps or social buttons that would set them. That is why we don't show you a banner asking you to accept them.

Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, may use strictly necessary technical cookies for the site's security (for example, to filter malicious traffic). These cookies do not require consent under article 22.2 of the Spanish LSSI-CE.

Links to WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook or Google Maps take you to those platforms, which apply their own cookie policies.

How to manage cookies

You can view and delete cookies in your browser settings (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge…).

Changes

If we add tools that use non-technical cookies in the future, we will update this policy and ask for your consent before setting them.
