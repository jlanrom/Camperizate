Ford Transit campervan conversion

The large Ford Transit is a very versatile camper base: easy to find, good on the road and available with front, rear or all-wheel drive.

Ask for information What's included

Why a Transit

  • L2H2, L3H2, L3H3 and L4H3 versions: there is one for every kind of traveller.
  • Front, rear or all-wheel drive (AWD) depending on version: choose according to whether you will stay on tarmac or go on tracks.
  • A good network of garages all over Spain for servicing.

Recommended layout

In a Transit L2H2 or L3H2, a layout with a full bathroom with shower and toilet next to the sliding door works very well, with the kitchen opposite, a dinette with approved seats behind the front seats and the bed at the back.

As with any large van, the equipment is that of our full conversion and we handle the homologation for you.

Frequently asked questions

Questions about converting a Ford Transit

Which Transit is best for a camper?

The front-wheel drive L2H2 or L3H2 is the most practical for two people. If you are tall, the H3 gives extra headroom.

Can a Transit have a full bathroom?

Yes. An L2H2 or larger has room for a bathroom with shower and toilet.
