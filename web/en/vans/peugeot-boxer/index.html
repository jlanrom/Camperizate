Peugeot Boxer campervan conversion

The Peugeot Boxer shares its body with the Fiat Ducato and the Citroën Jumper, so it has the same strengths: width for a transverse bed, a low floor and plenty of usable space. The photos show a 2023 Boxer we converted in our workshop.

A converted Boxer, inside

The Boxer in the photos is a 2023 2.2 BlueHDi 140 hp. We fitted it with a transverse double bed at the back, a tiled kitchen with a wooden worktop, a sofa with a side table, a roof vent with LED light, a TV, 200 Ah of lithium, a 400 W solar panel, 200 litres of water, a 115-litre Webasto fridge and Planar heating.

Boxer, Ducato or Jumper: which one?

Inside they are practically the same: same lengths (L1 to L4), same heights (H1 to H3) and the same width. The differences are in the engines, standard equipment, price and dealer network. Our advice: pick the one you find in the best condition at the best price; the conversion will be the same.

Things to bear in mind

  • Version: we recommend at least an L2H2 for standing headroom and space for a bathroom.
  • Factory windows: if your Boxer already has windows, we use them in the design; if not, we fit sliding glass windows with blinds.
  • Weight: with a standard car licence (category B) the limit is 3,500 kg. We design with the final weight in mind so you can travel fully loaded and legal.

Questions about converting a Peugeot Boxer

Is converting a Boxer the same as a Ducato?

Yes. The body and internal dimensions are the same, so the conversion and the price are too.

Which Boxer should I buy for a conversion?

An L2H2 or L3H2 with 140 hp or more if you will travel loaded or in the mountains. Message us before you buy and we will help you check it.
