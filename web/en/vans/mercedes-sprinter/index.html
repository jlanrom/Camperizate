Mercedes Sprinter campervan conversion

The Mercedes Sprinter is the premium van par excellence: build quality, reliability and a very high resale value. If you want a camper for many years and many miles, it is a safe bet.

Ask for information What's included

The Sprinter's strengths

  • Rear-wheel drive as standard on many versions (front-wheel and 4x4 also available): very good for towing and for traction when loaded.
  • Automatic gearbox available and top-level ride comfort.
  • Resale value: a converted, homologated Sprinter holds its price very well.

What to consider in the design

The Sprinter is narrower inside than the Ducato, so a transverse bed only works for people of average height. For taller people we usually plan a longitudinal bed or a layout with a rear bed and a convertible dinette.

In rear-wheel drive versions the floor is a little higher, which the roof height makes up for. And as with any large van, we keep an eye on the final weight so you do not exceed 3,500 kg if you drive on a standard car licence.

Frequently asked questions

Questions about converting a Mercedes Sprinter

Does a transverse bed fit in a Sprinter?

For people of average height, yes. If you are taller, we will recommend a longitudinal bed so you sleep well.

Rear or front-wheel drive for a Sprinter camper?

Rear-wheel drive is the most common and the best if you carry a lot of weight or tow. Front-wheel drive gives a slightly lower floor. 4x4 is for off-road use.
