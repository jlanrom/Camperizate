Before you choose

Why we recommend vans from L2H2

On large vans the letter L refers to length and H to roof height. An L2H2 (medium length, high roof) is the starting point we recommend: with an H2 roof you can stand up inside, and an L2 fits a double bed, full kitchen, bathroom and a dinette with approved seats.

If more than two of you travel, or you want a bigger bathroom or a large rear garage, an L3 or L4 gives you plenty of room. Just bear in mind parking in town and the total weight.

Weight: the key almost nobody looks at

With a standard car licence (category B) you can drive vehicles up to 3,500 kg maximum authorised mass. A full conversion adds weight (furniture, water, batteries…), so we design every camper with the final weight in mind: lithium batteries, light boards and well-placed tanks so you can travel loaded and within the law.
