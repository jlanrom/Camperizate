Opel Movano campervan conversion

The Opel Movano is a special case: depending on the year, it is a different van inside. Knowing which one you have is key to designing the camper properly.

Ask for information What's included

Two very different Movanos

  • Movano from 2021: shares its body with the Fiat Ducato, Peugeot Boxer and Citroën Jumper. Front-wheel drive, same dimensions and the width that allows a transverse bed.
  • Movano up to 2021: a twin of the Renault Master (and the Nissan NV400). It came with front or rear-wheel drive, and its interior is a little narrower, so the layout changes.

They are easy to tell apart from the front, but if in doubt, send us the registration or year and we will check.

How we design the camper for each model

On the current Movano we use the Ducato-family layout: transverse rear bed, full kitchen and bathroom. On the older one we decide with you between a longitudinal bed or a shorter transverse bed, depending on your height and how much room you want for the bathroom and lounge.

Either way, the equipment is that of our full conversion: Kaiflex insulation, marine HPL furniture, 200 Ah of lithium with 400 W of solar, 200 litres of water, kitchen, bathroom, heating and homologation.

Frequently asked questions

Questions about converting a Opel Movano

How do I know which Movano I have?

If it is from 2021 onwards, it is normally the Ducato-based version. Send us a photo or the registration year and we will check.

Does a transverse bed fit in a Movano?

In the current Movano, yes, just like a Ducato. In the older one (Master-based) it depends on your height; we look at it together at the design stage.
