Fiat Ducato campervan conversion

The Fiat Ducato is the most popular base for campervans in Europe, and for good reason: it is wide, front-wheel drive and has a low, flat floor. A perfect starting point for a full conversion.

Ask for information What's included

Why the Ducato makes such a good camper

Its big advantage is interior width: it is one of the few vans where a double bed fits transversely, from side to side. That frees up more than half a metre of length for the kitchen, the bathroom or a more comfortable lounge.

Front-wheel drive keeps the load floor low and free of a transmission tunnel, so it is easy to step in and you gain headroom. And as the base of most motorhomes, you will find parts and garages anywhere in Europe.

Which version to choose

  • L2H2: the best balance. Transverse bed, kitchen, compact bathroom and a dinette with approved seats, while still easy to drive in town.
  • L3H2: about 60 cm more. Room for a bigger bathroom, more storage or a rear garage for bikes.
  • L4H2: for a fixed bed plus a separate lounge, or for more than two travellers.
  • H3: if you are tall or want overhead lockers without ducking.

Frequently asked questions

Questions about converting a Fiat Ducato

Which Ducato is best for a campervan?

For two people, the L2H2 is the best balance between living space and ease of driving and parking. If you want a bigger bathroom or a rear garage, look at the L3H2.

Does a transverse double bed fit in a Ducato?

Yes. It is one of its big advantages: the interior width lets the bed run from side to side, leaving more space for the rest of the camper.
