Buying a used Jumper to convert

Many customers come to us with a used Jumper, and it is a great choice if you pick well. This is what we recommend checking before you buy:

  • Version and size: make sure it is an H2 (high roof) and ideally an L2 or L3. The registration document shows it.
  • Rust: check the underside, wheel arches, door edges and the roof where the roof vents and solar panel will go.
  • Maintenance: service history, mileage and the state of the clutch and timing belt.
  • Windows and bulkhead: a van with factory windows or rear seats can save work; the cargo bulkhead is usually removed.
  • Gross weight: check the maximum authorised mass (usually 3,500 kg for a standard car licence).
