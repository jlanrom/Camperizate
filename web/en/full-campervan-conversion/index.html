What the conversion includes

Full campervan conversion

Everything you need to travel off-grid in comfort, built and homologated in our Málaga workshop. This is our standard specification, area by area.

  • Homologation included
  • Approved seats
  • Warranty
  • Ready in 3 months

Standard specification

Area by area

01

Insulation and panelling

  • Double insulation of the whole van: 20 mm Kaiflex on the panels and 10 mm on the ribs.
  • White tongue-and-groove panelling on ceiling and walls; pine panelling on the doors.
  • 15 mm marine poplar floor with AC5 laminate.
02

Furniture

  • Complete furniture in marine HPL, resistant to damp and daily use.
  • Wall-mounted table, outdoor table and small fold-down table.
03

Power and electrics

  • 2 × 100 Ah lithium batteries (200 Ah in total).
  • 400 W NDS high-profile solar panel with MPPT controller.
  • 1000 W pure sine wave inverter/charger.
  • CBE PC180 control panel.
  • Several 12 V sockets, outdoor 230 V socket and plenty of lighting points.
04

Water

  • 200 l fresh water tank with Shurflo pressure pump.
  • Whale / Webasto gas water heater.
  • Outdoor shower with hot and cold water.
05

Kitchen

  • Kitchen unit with sink and Dometic two-burner hob.
  • 115 l Webasto Isotherm compressor fridge.
06

Bathroom

  • Bathroom cubicle with joinery door, shower tray and tap.
  • Turbovent roof vent in the bathroom.
07

Ventilation, light and windows

  • 2 Dometic roof vents: one in the lounge and one over the bed.
  • 2 sliding glass windows with blinds and curtains.
08

Sleeping and heating

  • 1 or 2 full double beds, 135 × 185 cm.
  • Planar diesel night heater.
09

Safety and outdoors

  • Reversing camera.
  • Outdoor light and outdoor 230 V socket.
10

Homologation (road-legal paperwork)

  • Approved rear seats so you can travel legally.
  • Full homologation project for the Spanish ITV, handled by us.

Frequently asked questions

What people ask us most

Does the price include the van?

No. The price (from €24,900) is for the complete conversion of your van. If you don't have one yet, we'll help you find the right base.

Why does the price vary?

The basic specification costs €24,900 on L2H2 and L3H2 vans and €26,000 on L4H2. With more advanced equipment it goes up to €26,300. Homologation is always included.

Which vans do you work with?

We focus on large vans from L2H2: Fiat Ducato, Peugeot Boxer, Citroën Jumper, Opel Movano, Renault Master, Volkswagen Crafter, Mercedes Sprinter, Ford Transit and Iveco Daily.

How long does it take?

About 3 months from booking to handing over your camper, finished and homologated.

Is homologation included?

Yes. We prepare and handle the full homologation project for the Spanish ITV, including approved rear seats so you can travel legally.

Can the equipment be customised?

Yes. The specification is a guide and is adapted to your van and the way you travel. The final price depends on the configuration we agree.

How do I pay?

A €1,000 deposit to book your production slot, 40% when work starts, 40% once most of the work is done and the remaining 20% on delivery.
